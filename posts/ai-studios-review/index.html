How to Make Free AI Videos (2026): Step-by-Step Beginner Guide

Want to make videos without a camera, a microphone, or editing skills? AI video tools can turn a few lines of text into a finished video in minutes — and several of them are free to start. This guide shows you exactly how, step by step.

How to make a free AI video in 5 steps

Step 1 — Write a short script

Decide one message per video. Keep it under 150 words for a 60-second video. Write like you talk: short sentences, one idea at a time.

Step 2 — Pick a free tool

For a talking presenter, start with AI Studios. For quick social clips, CapCut. For creative text-to-video scenes, PixVerse. See the comparison below.

Step 3 — Paste your script and choose an avatar or style

Most tools offer ready-made AI avatars and voices. Pick one that fits your topic, paste your script, and preview the voice.

Step 4 — Generate and review

Generate the video, watch it fully, and fix awkward lines. Short videos are cheap to regenerate on free credits — don't settle for the first draft.

Step 5 — Export and post

Export in 1080p where the free plan allows, add captions if the tool doesn't, and post natively to TikTok, Reels or Shorts for the best reach.

Tips that make free AI videos look pro

  • One idea per video. Short beats long.
  • Write scripts out loud first — if it sounds odd, rewrite it.
  • Add captions: most viewers watch muted.
  • Keep the same avatar and style across a series — it builds a brand.
  • Export at the highest quality your free plan allows.

Frequently asked questions

Can I really make AI videos for free?

Yes. CapCut, PixVerse, Google Vids and AI Studios all offer free tiers or trials. Free plans have limits (credits, video length, watermark on some tools), but they are enough to learn and to publish real content.

Do I need a camera or recording skills?

No. With text-to-video and AI avatars, you type a script and the AI speaks and presents it for you. You never appear on camera.

Which free AI video tool is best for beginners?

CapCut's free AI video generator is the easiest start. For realistic AI presenters, AI Studios leads. For short creative clips, try PixVerse.

Can I use free AI videos commercially?

It depends on the tool's free-plan licence. Most free tiers allow personal and social-media use; check each tool's current terms before using videos in paid ads or client work.

How long can a free AI video be?

It varies: Google Vids gives about 60 seconds of AI clips per month on personal accounts, PixVerse clips are a few seconds each, and CapCut's free tier is more generous for short social videos.
