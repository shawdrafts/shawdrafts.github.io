7 Email Subject Line Formulas That Get Opened

Your subject line decides whether an email lives or dies. These 7 formulas are proven open-rate lifters — steal them, adapt them, test them.

1. Curiosity

"You're doing this wrong (and it's costing you)"

Open a loop the reader must close. Never clickbait past the payoff — deliver in the email.

2. Benefit

"Get 30% more replies with this one tweak"

Lead with the outcome, not the feature.

3. Question

"Still struggling with low open rates?"

A question about their pain earns the open.

4. Numbers

"7 subject lines that doubled our opens"

Specific numbers beat vague promises.

5. Urgency

"Offer ends tonight — last chance"

Use only when genuinely true, or trust dies.

6. Personal

"Ahmed, a quick idea for your store"

First names and specifics feel one-to-one.

7. How-to

"How to write subject lines people actually open"

Promise a skill, then teach it in the email.

Rules that apply to all 7

  • Keep it under 50 characters where possible — mobile cuts the rest.
  • One idea per subject line.
  • A/B test: send two versions to 10% each, then the winner to the rest.
  • Match the subject to the email body — mismatch kills trust and future opens.
