How to Turn Followers Into Customers

10,000 followers and zero sales is a vanity trap. Here's the simple funnel that turns attention into orders — no ad budget required.

The 4-step funnel

  1. Attract with value. Teach, entertain, or solve — every post should give before it asks.
  2. Build trust. Show your face, your process, your results, your reviews. People buy from people they trust.
  3. Make the offer obvious. Link in bio, pinned post, story highlights — a follower should find how to buy in under 10 seconds.
  4. Follow up. Most sales need 5–7 touches. Email, retargeting, DMs, repeat content — don't stop at one post.

Mistakes that kill conversion

  • Posting only promotions — followers tune out fast.
  • No clear next step — "link in bio" beats "DM for details" every time.
  • Hiding prices — mystery doesn't create demand, it creates friction.

Your 7-day action plan

  • Day 1–3: post 3 value posts (tips, before/after, behind the scenes).
  • Day 4: post one soft offer with a clear link.
  • Day 5–6: share a testimonial or result.
  • Day 7: ask a question — replies boost reach and start conversations that become sales.
