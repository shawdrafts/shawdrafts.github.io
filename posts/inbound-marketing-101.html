Inbound Marketing 101: Stop Chasing, Start Attracting

October 24, 2026

Most small businesses market the hard way: cold messages, spammy ads, and posts nobody asked for. That is outbound marketing — interrupting people and hoping someone listens. Inbound marketing flips it around: you create genuinely useful content, and the right customers come to you.

The inbound method runs on a simple flywheel with three stages: Attract, Engage, Delight.

1. Attract: pull the right people in

Attract means earning attention instead of buying it. Blog posts that answer real buyer questions, helpful social content, and SEO-optimized pages do this work around the clock. The rule is simple: create content your ideal customer would search for even if you never sold them anything.

2. Engage: turn visitors into leads

Traffic alone pays no bills. Engage means converting visitors into leads by offering value in exchange for contact — a useful guide, a free checklist, a helpful email sequence. The key: every offer must be worth more than the email address it asks for.

3. Delight: turn customers into promoters

The flywheel keeps spinning when happy customers recommend you. Fast support, thoughtful follow-ups, and content that keeps helping after the sale turn one-time buyers into your best marketing channel — word of mouth.

Where to start this week

  • Write down the 5 questions your customers ask most — those are your first 5 content topics.
  • Pick one channel (blog, Instagram, or email) and publish there consistently for 90 days.
  • Add one clear call to action to every piece of content you publish.

Inbound is slower than blasting ads, but it compounds. Every helpful article keeps attracting buyers long after you publish it. Stop chasing customers — start deserving their attention.
