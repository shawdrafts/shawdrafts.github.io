What Is Inbound Marketing? A Beginner's Guide

Inbound marketing is the art of getting customers to come to you — instead of chasing them with ads. You create helpful content, people find it, trust builds, and sales follow.

The 3 stages

1. Attract. Blog posts, social content and SEO bring the right people to you. They are already looking for answers — you just have to be the best answer.

2. Engage. Once they arrive, you help them: lead magnets, email sequences, live chat, helpful replies. You solve problems before asking for money.

3. Delight. After the sale, great service turns customers into promoters who refer others — the cheapest marketing there is.

Why it beats cold ads for small businesses

  • Content keeps working after you publish it — ads stop the moment you stop paying.
  • People trust a helpful article more than a banner.
  • It compounds: each post adds to your search presence.

Start this week

  • Write one post answering your customers' #1 question.
  • Share it on one social channel, consistently.
  • Add one way for readers to contact you (form, WhatsApp, Fiverr link).
