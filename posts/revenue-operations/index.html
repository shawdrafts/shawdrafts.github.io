What Is Revenue Operations (RevOps)?

RevOps is the discipline of making marketing, sales and customer service work as one revenue machine — same data, same goals, no finger-pointing.

The problem RevOps solves

Marketing celebrates leads. Sales complains the leads are bad. Service inherits unhappy customers. Everyone optimizes their own number — and revenue still stalls. RevOps fixes the seams between teams.

The 4 pillars

  • One data source. A single CRM truth — no spreadsheet wars.
  • One funnel. Shared definitions: what counts as a lead, a qualified opportunity, a win.
  • One process. Handoffs with owners and SLAs, not hope.
  • One scoreboard. Revenue, not vanity metrics, is the shared target.

RevOps for a small team (or a solo freelancer)

You don't need enterprise software. You need: one place for contacts, one pipeline view, one weekly revenue review, and one rule — every lead gets a next step and an owner. That's RevOps at freelancer scale, and it's how I run ShawDrafts.
