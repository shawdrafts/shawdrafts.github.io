What Is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

October 26, 2026

SEO was about ranking in a list of blue links. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) is about becoming the single answer that AI assistants and answer engines quote directly. When someone asks "how much does a logo design cost?", the engine picks one source to summarize — AEO is the discipline of being that source.

Why it matters now

More searches than ever end without a click: the user reads the AI-generated answer and moves on. If your content is not structured to be quoted, you are invisible in that growing share of searches. Traditional SEO still matters, but AEO is the layer on top that captures zero-click visibility.

How to optimize for answers

  • Structure content as Q&A. Use clear question headings ("What is...?", "How do I...?") followed by direct answers. Answer engines love this format.
  • Answer first, explain after. Put the direct answer in the first 1–2 sentences under each heading, then add detail below. If the engine only quotes your first paragraph, it should still be complete.
  • Be specific and factual. Vague content never gets quoted. Numbers, steps, definitions, and comparisons are the material answer engines cite.
  • Keep content fresh. Answer engines prefer current information. Review and update your key pages regularly.

AEO and SEO work together

Good news: most AEO best practices are also good SEO. Clear headings, direct answers, and factual depth help you rank in classic search and get quoted by AI. You are not choosing between them — you are writing content that serves both.

The businesses that learn to "be the answer" now will own the next era of search visibility. Start with your five most-asked customer questions, and answer each one better than anyone else on the internet.
