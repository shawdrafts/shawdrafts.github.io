What Is AEO? Answer Engine Optimization Explained

People don't just Google anymore — they ask ChatGPT, voice assistants and AI Overviews. AEO is how your content becomes the answer those engines quote.

SEO vs AEO

SEO ranks your page in a list of links. AEO gets your content quoted as the direct answer. Both matter — but AEO is where search is heading.

How to optimize for answers

  • Answer the question in the first 2–3 sentences, plainly.
  • Use clear headings phrased as questions people actually ask.
  • Add FAQ sections and FAQ schema (like this site does).
  • Keep facts accurate and cite sources — AI engines prefer trustworthy pages.
  • Structure content so it can be lifted: short paragraphs, lists, tables.

Does AEO replace SEO?

No. AEO builds on SEO: you still need crawlable pages, good titles and real authority. Think of AEO as SEO's next layer — the part that speaks AI's language.
